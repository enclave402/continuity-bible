SIX HOURS (Six Hours, Theo first): 5 spreads, read in order.
Each spread has a picture; the PICTURE line says what it shows.

=== SPREAD 1 OF 5 ===
PART: Part one: six hours
TITLE: Across town
PICTURE: A man alone in a carpenter's workshop under a hanging bulb, a phone face down beside the plane, a brass key on a nail by the door.
TIME: Thursday, 11:55 PM

Across town Theo turned his phone face down on the workbench and picked up the plane again. The board did not need planing.

On its nail by the door hung the brass key, where it always hung. He had not locked up. He was not leaving yet.

MARGIN NOTE: I was not ignoring her. I was ignoring the part of me that wanted to answer. — Theo (looking back)

=== SPREAD 2 OF 5 ===
PART: Part two: morning
TITLE: Morning at the workbench
PICTURE: Theo in the workshop doorway at dawn, the brass key in his hand, grey light on the sawdust.
TIME: Friday, 6:10 AM

At six Theo wrote a reply, read it twice, and deleted it. Then he wrote a shorter one and deleted that.

He took the brass key off its nail and locked the shop behind him, which he had never once forgotten to do.

MARGIN NOTE: Deleting a sentence felt safer than sending one. It was not safer. It was just quieter. — Theo (looking back)

=== SPREAD 3 OF 5 ===
PART: Part one: six hours
TITLE: The porch
PICTURE: Two women on a narrow front porch at night, one bent over a glowing phone, one at the door with her keys out.
TIME: Thursday, 11:50 PM

Chloe dug through her bag for her keys while Sam read the message again under the porch bulb. It was the fourth time since the cab.

Six hours. He had opened it at 11:52 and said nothing. Sam knew the minute the way you know a birthday.

DIALOGUE:
  Sam: He read it. Look, it says read.
  Chloe: I see it. Come inside.

MARGIN NOTE: I counted the hours like they were evidence. They were not evidence. They were hours. — Sam (looking back)

=== SPREAD 4 OF 5 ===
PART: Part one: six hours
TITLE: Read at 11:52
PICTURE: Samantha Hayes at a kitchen table under lamplight, the cracked phone glowing between two mugs, Chloe across from her.
TIME: Thursday, 11:58 PM

At the kitchen table Samantha laid the phone face up between the mugs, like a card she had been dealt and could not put down.

Theodore Vance signed his contracts with his whole name. He signed her with one letter.

DIALOGUE:
  Sam: Chlo, is this a red flag?
  Chloe: It is a text. Read me what it says.
  Theo: Running late. Talk tmrw. –T

MARGIN NOTE: One letter can hold a whole person, or hide one. I did not know yet which. — Chloe

FOOTNOTE: A read receipt says a screen was open. It does not say a person was ready.

=== SPREAD 5 OF 5 ===
PART: Part two: morning
TITLE: The text, sent
PICTURE: Three pictures in a row: a thumb over the send button of a cracked phone; the sent message alone on a screen; Theo reading it standing at the workbench.
TIME: Friday, 7:30 AM

Sam typed it on the porch where she had read his, so the two messages would at least share a place.

Nine miles away a phone turned face up on a workbench. Theo read it standing, the way he read invoices.

DIALOGUE:
  Sam: I'd like to know where I stand. You can tell me tonight, or you can tell me now.

MARGIN NOTE: I sent the thing I was afraid of. The fear came along. It did not get to drive. — Sam (looking back)

FOOTNOTE: Saying where you stand is not a demand. It is a map with your own position marked on it.
