# Approval steps

*Also called:* asset lifecycle, approval workflow, review status, draft, approved, rejected

**What it is:** The stages a picture moves through, such as draft, approved and rejected, and what has to be true to move to the next one.

**Why it matters:** Without clear steps, a draft ends up on the live page, or a rejected picture comes back.

**The rule here:** A picture moves forward only when a check passes. The checker is not the maker. A rejected picture is kept with its reason, not deleted.

**How to use it:** Give each picture a status field. Write down what moves it from one status to the next.

**Next step:** Pick one picture and write down its status and why.

## How it connects

Each step should be guarded by a [quality gate](GATE.md), and the check should be done by a [second checker](SECOND-CHECKER.md), not the maker.
